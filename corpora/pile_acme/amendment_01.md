# AMENDMENT NO. 1

**To:** the Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2026
**Between:** Brightwell Manufacturing Inc. and Acme Fabrication Services LLC

This Amendment No. 1 is effective as of 1 June 2026.

## 1. Rate Adjustment

Section 2 of the Agreement is amended so that the standard hourly rate is
USD 135 per hour, effective for all services performed on or after the effective
date of this Amendment.

## 2. Limitation of Liability

Section 4 of the Agreement is amended so that the aggregate liability cap is
USD 500,000.

## 3. No Other Changes

All other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.

Signed for Brightwell Manufacturing Inc. — R. Calloway, VP Operations
Signed for Acme Fabrication Services LLC — D. Okonjo, Managing Director
