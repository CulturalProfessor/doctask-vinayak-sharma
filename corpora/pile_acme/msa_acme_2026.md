# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Between:** Brightwell Manufacturing Inc. ("Client")
**And:** Acme Fabrication Services LLC ("Supplier")

This Master Services Agreement is entered into and effective as of 1 March 2026.

## 1. Term

The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months from the effective
date. This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve-month terms
unless either party gives notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 6.

## 2. Services and Rates

Supplier shall provide fabrication and finishing services as described in one or
more Statements of Work. Services are billed at a standard rate of USD 120 per
hour unless a Statement of Work specifies otherwise.

## 3. Invoicing and Payment

Supplier shall invoice Client monthly in arrears. Invoices are payable within
thirty (30) days of receipt. Amounts not paid when due accrue interest at 1.0%
per month.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

Neither party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall exceed
USD 250,000.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using no less than
reasonable care, for a period of three (3) years following disclosure.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement, or give notice of non-renewal, upon
sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
