# AMENDMENT NO. 2

**To:** the Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2026
**Between:** Brightwell Manufacturing Inc. and Acme Fabrication Services LLC

This Amendment No. 2 is effective as of 1 December 2026.

## 1. Rate Adjustment

Section 2 of the Agreement, as previously amended, is further amended so that
the standard hourly rate is USD 145 per hour, effective for all services
performed on or after the effective date of this Amendment.

## 2. Termination

Section 6 of the Agreement is amended so that either party may terminate, or
give notice of non-renewal, upon ninety (90) days prior written notice.

## 3. No Other Changes

All other terms and conditions of the Agreement, as amended, remain in full
force and effect.

Signed for Brightwell Manufacturing Inc. — R. Calloway, VP Operations
Signed for Acme Fabrication Services LLC — D. Okonjo, Managing Director
