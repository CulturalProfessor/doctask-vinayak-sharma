STATEMENT OF WORK — SOW-221

Issued under the Master Services Agreement dated 1 January 2026 between Northwind Logistics Group PLC and Harbourline Freight Systems LLC.

1. Services Ordered

Supplier will provide drayage between the Port of Elizabeth and Client's Bayonne cross-dock, including container pickup, chassis management and customs documentation for import loads.

2. Period

Services run from 1 February 2026 to 31 December 2026.

3. Authorised Effort

Supplier may bill up to six hundred (600) hours against this Statement of Work. Hours beyond that ceiling require a written change order agreed in advance by both parties.

4. Service Levels

  • Container collected within 4 hours of availability notice, 95% of loads
  • Customs documentation lodged same business day, 98% of loads
  • Monthly service review, first Tuesday of the following month

5. Value

The total value of this Statement of Work is USD 57,000.

6. Charges Not Covered

Demurrage, per-diem chassis charges and port congestion fees are passed through at cost and do not count against the hours ceiling in Section 3.
