# AMENDMENT NO. 1

**To:** the Master Services Agreement dated 1 January 2026
**Between:** Northwind Logistics Group PLC and Harbourline Freight Systems LLC

Effective 1 August 2026.

## 1. Revised Charges

Article III of the Agreement is amended so that chargeable work is billed at
USD 110 per hour with effect from the effective date of this Amendment. The
revision reflects the fuel surcharge and driver wage adjustments notified to
Client on 12 June 2026.

## 2. Scope of the Revision

No change is made to settlement terms, to the notice periods in Article VII, or
to the insurance obligations in Article V. Pass-through port charges continue to
be billed at cost.

## 3. Continuation

Save as amended above, the Agreement continues in full force and effect.

Executed for Northwind Logistics Group PLC — M. Adeyemi, Director of Supply Chain
Executed for Harbourline Freight Systems LLC — P. Vasquez, Chief Operating Officer
