# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Client:** Northwind Logistics Group PLC
**Supplier:** Harbourline Freight Systems LLC

Effective 1 January 2026.

## Article I — Engagement

Supplier will provide inland drayage, container handling and customs
documentation services to Client's east coast distribution network, as ordered
through Statements of Work issued under this Agreement.

## Article II — Term and Renewal

The initial term runs for twelve (12) months from the effective date. The
Agreement renews for successive twelve-month terms unless either party serves
notice under Article VII.

## Article III — Charges

Chargeable work is billed at USD 95 per hour. Rates are held for the initial
term and may be revised only by written amendment executed by both parties.
Pass-through port charges are billed at cost and are outside the hourly rate.

## Article IV — Settlement

Supplier issues invoices monthly in arrears. Client shall settle each invoice
within forty-five (45) days of receipt. Sums outstanding beyond that period
carry interest at 0.75% per month.

## Article V — Insurance and Liability

Supplier shall maintain cargo liability insurance of not less than USD 2,000,000
per occurrence and shall name Client as an additional insured.

Each party is responsible for loss or damage caused by its own negligence, to
the extent determined under applicable law. Nothing in this Agreement limits
liability for fraud or for death or personal injury.

## Article VI — Confidentiality

Neither party shall disclose the other's commercial terms to a third party
without written consent, during the term and for two (2) years afterwards.

## Article VII — Notice of Termination or Non-Renewal

Either party may terminate this Agreement, or give notice that it will not
renew, on ninety (90) days written notice to the other party.

## Article VIII — Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey.
